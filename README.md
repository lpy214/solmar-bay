**Solmar Bay** is an original open-world crime game that runs in a desktop browser from plain
static files — no build step, no network requests, no libraries.

Dante Vega is back in Solmar Bay: broke, on foot, and out of options. Explore a sun-soaked
coastal city — road grid, beach, harbour, park, traffic lights — on foot or behind the wheel.
Steal any car (press E), outrun patrol cars, raise a wanted level from 0 to 5 stars, and work
through five story missions for the crew: steal a getaway car, deliver a package across town
against the clock, lay low until a 3-star heat dies down, grab six cash drops, and pull the
final harbour heist. Punch and shoot (mouse aim, click or Space), grab health and ammo
pickups, spend cash on repairs and bullets, and watch it all on a HUD with a live minimap.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

Controls: WASD / arrows to move and drive · mouse to aim · click or Space to shoot ·
F to punch · E to steal / exit a car · H to buy health ($50) · J to buy ammo ($30) ·
P or Esc to pause · M to mute. Best cash and mission progress save in the browser.
